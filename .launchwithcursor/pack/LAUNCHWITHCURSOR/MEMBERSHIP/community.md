# Community

Founder Launch members get access to a private cohort channel for async questions, wins, and peer support.

---

## Where to find your cohort

Your onboarding email includes the invite link (WhatsApp, Slack, or Discord — depending on your cohort).

Lost the link? Submit a quick note on [Mentorship & fair usage](/guides/mentorship) and we will resend it.

---

## What the community is for

- Sharing progress and launch milestones
- Quick tips that help other founders
- Celebrating first deploy and first paying user

---

## What to use mentorship for instead

- Debugging your specific codebase
- Architecture decisions for your app
- Deploy failures with logs
- Booking a deep-dive session

See the [fair usage policy](./fair-usage-policy.md) for limits on 1:1 support.

---

## House rules

1. Be kind — everyone is learning
2. No unsolicited DMs selling services
3. Do not paste secrets, API keys, or customer data
4. Search the channel before asking duplicate questions

Welcome — we are glad you are building with us.
