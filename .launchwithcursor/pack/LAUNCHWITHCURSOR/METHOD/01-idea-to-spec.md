# Phase 0 — Idea to MVP Spec

**Where this happens:** Inside your LaunchWithCursor member dashboard.

**Output:** One file: `MVP-SPEC.md` — **no code**, only product and structure.

---

## What to do

1. Open the **Idea to MVP spec** step inside LaunchWithCursor
2. Describe:
   - what you want to build
   - who it is for
   - the problem it solves
   - what success looks like
   - any important constraints
3. Generate your spec inside the platform
4. Review the result carefully and cut anything that is too ambitious for an MVP
5. Save the final version of the spec before moving on
6. Copy or download the final spec as `MVP-SPEC.md` for your project

---

## What your spec should contain

Your spec should give you:

1. Product summary
2. Target user
3. Problem to solve
4. Must Have features
5. Nice to Have (after launch)
6. NOT in MVP
7. User flows
8. Technical shape in plain English
9. Success criteria

Still **no code**. The goal is clarity, not implementation.

---

## Review checklist

1. Read the **NOT in MVP** section carefully
2. Make sure the Must Have list is small enough to ship fast
3. Share the idea with a trusted person and ask: **Would you pay for this?**

---

## Done when

- [ ] `MVP-SPEC.md` committed to your repo (or saved before repo exists)
- [ ] Must Have list has **≤ 8 features**
- [ ] NOT in MVP list is explicit (native app, admin analytics, etc.)
- [ ] The scope is small enough to launch quickly
