# LaunchWithCursor Method — Start Here

## What is Cursor?

Cursor is an AI-powered code editor that helps you build software by working with AI inside your codebase. Instead of writing every line of code manually, you describe what you want, and Cursor helps generate, edit, debug, and improve your application.

This dramatically lowers the technical barrier to building software, making it possible for founders and domain experts, not just professional developers, to build real products.

## Who LaunchWithCursor is for

LaunchWithCursor is designed primarily for founders, entrepreneurs, consultants, business owners, researchers, and other domain experts who have valuable ideas but do not have traditional software engineering skills.

It is for people who want to build and launch their own MVPs using AI instead of hiring expensive development teams from day one, while still having access to proven guidance, technical best practices, and expert support when needed.

## Our goal

Our goal is to help non-technical founders turn their ideas into production-ready MVPs and launch them as quickly as possible using AI.

LaunchWithCursor provides the infrastructure, AI-native development guides, best practices, and technical mentorship needed to remove the traditional barriers to building software, so founders can focus on validating their ideas, learning from users, and acquiring customers instead of getting stuck on technology.

---

## What kinds of products is LaunchWithCursor for?

LaunchWithCursor is designed to help founders build and launch MVPs quickly — usually web-based apps that run in a browser on desktops, tablets, and phones. **MVP-SPEC.md defines the mobile strategy** (PWA, native app, or desktop-only); the starter pack must not override it.

For many startups, a web or PWA MVP is the fastest way to validate an idea. If your spec calls for native iOS/Android instead, follow MVP-SPEC — the platform still hosts web/API services; native apps may use a separate build pipeline.

**Great fit for LaunchWithCursor:**

- Marketing websites
- Landing pages
- Portfolio websites
- SaaS applications
- Client and customer portals
- Internal business tools
- Marketplaces/E-commerce web-apps
- Booking and scheduling platforms
- CRM and ERP systems
- AI-powered applications
- Learning management systems (LMS)
- Community and membership platforms
- Workflow and productivity tools
- Dashboards and analytics platforms
- Blogs/Content web-apps

**Not the right fit (at least initially):**

- Native iOS or Android apps as the first version of your product
- Applications built with technology stacks outside the LaunchWithCursor ecosystem

By focusing on a proven, AI-friendly technology stack, we help founders spend less time on technical complexity and more time validating their ideas, acquiring users, and growing their businesses.

## What LaunchWithCursor gives you

- **Launch infrastructure** — push to GitHub and deploy your app live
- **Code visibility** — database diagram, API map, backend logic explanation, plain-English explanations
- **Guides + Cursor rules** — opinionated stack so you don't have to make architecture decisions
- **Technical mentorship** — get help by message when you are stuck, plus scheduled sessions ([fair usage policy](../MEMBERSHIP/fair-usage-policy.md))
